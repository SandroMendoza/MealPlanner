Meal Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Meal Planner is a meal planning app that keeps your data on your device. It has no user accounts and no analytics or crash reporting. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described under “Ads”. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, spending entries, profile (name, bio and photo) and settings in its private storage on your device. When Android backup is turned on, Android includes this data, photos included, in your device's backup to your Google account, and copies it when you move to a new device. Google stores the backup under its own privacy policy, and the developer has no access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from your device. You can manage or delete backups in your device's backup settings.

Photos

When you add a recipe or profile photo, the app uses the Android photo picker, so it only sees the photos you select. The app saves a smaller copy of each photo, without its location or other metadata. Photos are never uploaded, except in your device's own backup described above.

Meal reminders

If you turn on meal reminders, your device schedules and shows them. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you export a spending report, the app passes the file to the app you choose in the Android share menu. That app's own privacy policy covers what happens next. When you save a backup file under Settings, it holds your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, settings and photos, and is saved only where you choose; the app does not send it anywhere.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech service installed on your device. The text being read is handled by that service under its provider's privacy policy.

Voice assistants

On supported Android versions, assistant apps on your device can read and update your shopping list, recipes, meal plan and spending report when you ask them to. The assistant handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Ads

The home and meal plan screens show banner ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on that IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Your recipes, meal plans and other app data are never shared with AdMob or sent to the developer.

Children

Meal Planner is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy is updated and the date above changes with it.
