Meal Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Meal Planner is a meal planning app that keeps your data on your device. It has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or crash reporting. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, spending entries, profile (name, bio and photo) and settings in its private storage on your device. This data is not included in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Photos

When you add a recipe or profile photo, the app uses the Android photo picker, so it only sees the photos you select. Those photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Meal reminders

If you turn on meal reminders, your device schedules and shows them. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you export a spending report, the app passes the file to the app you choose in the Android share menu. That app's own privacy policy covers what happens next.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech service installed on your device. The text being read is handled by that service under its provider's privacy policy.

Voice assistants

On supported Android versions, assistant apps on your device can read and update your shopping list, recipes, meal plan and spending report when you ask them to. The assistant handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Children

Meal Planner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy is updated and the date above changes with it.
